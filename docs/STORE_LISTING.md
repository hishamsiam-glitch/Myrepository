## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Scientific Calculator is a clean, fast, and completely private calculator
for everyday math and advanced scientific work.

BASIC CALCULATOR
• Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division
• Percentages, sign toggle, parentheses
• Large, readable display with a live result preview as you type

SCIENTIFIC MODE
• Trigonometric functions: sin, cos, tan, and their inverses
• Logarithms: natural log (ln) and base-10 log (log)
• Powers, square roots, squares, and reciprocals (1/x)
• Factorials and the constants π and e
• Switch between degrees and radians with one tap

DESIGNED TO GET OUT OF YOUR WAY
• Clean Material You interface that adapts to light and dark mode
• Clear error messages instead of crashes (e.g. divide-by-zero)
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no account required

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Scientific Calculator requests zero permissions and makes zero network
requests. Every calculation happens entirely on your device. Nothing is
collected, stored, or shared. See the full privacy policy for details.

Whether you need a quick everyday calculation or a scientific function for
homework, work, or study, Scientific Calculator gets you the answer
quickly and accurately.
```
