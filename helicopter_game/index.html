Skyline Heli

Fly over oceans, forests, deserts and mountain ranges.

  • Hold a finger anywhere on the screen to move forward. Lift it to hover.
  • Tilt your phone left / right to turn.
  • Tip the top of the phone away to descend, towards you to climb.
  • Stay above the ground: the ALT gauge shows terrain height under you.
  • Fly low over fuel cans to refuel; pass through rings at their altitude for points.

Hold the phone the way you want to fly, then tap Start: that pose becomes neutral. Press ⟳ in game to reset it. Best: 0

No tilt sensor? Drag on the screen to steer, or use arrow keys + space.
