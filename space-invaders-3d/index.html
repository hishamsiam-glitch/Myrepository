HOW TO PLAY

PHONE — tap ENABLE TILT CONTROLS, hold the phone comfortably, then tilt left / right to steer. Tilt forward / back to move the ship up and down the lane. Tap the screen to fire; hold to keep firing. Use ⌖ CENTRE to re-calibrate.

NO SENSORS? — drag a finger across the screen to steer, tap to fire.

KEYBOARD — ← → or A / D steer, ↑ ↓ or W / S move in depth, SPACE fires, P pauses.

GOAL — clear all 100 levels. The invaders speed up as their numbers thin, drop closer every time they touch a side, and win if they reach your lane. A boss saucer guards every 10th level. Armoured (2 hits) and phantom (3 hits) invaders appear in later sectors.

SCORE — points are multiplied by the sector number (levels 1-10 ×1 … 91-100 ×10). Every game is written to the score log; the top ten enter the high-score table with your three initials, just like the arcade.
