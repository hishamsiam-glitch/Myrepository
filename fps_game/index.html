How to play

Hold the phone in landscape in front of you like a window into the game. Tap ⟳ whenever the neutral pose feels off.

Turn / aimRotate the phone left or right, like turning your body.
WalkTilt scheme: tip the top edge away from you to walk forward, towards you to back up.
Gyro look scheme: hold a finger on the left half; slide it down to back up.
Touch scheme: left-half joystick.
StrafeTilt the phone left or right (right edge down = step right).
Look up / downOnly in the Gyro look scheme: tilt the phone up or down. In Tilt the aim stays level and hostiles are engaged at eye height.
FireHold the FIRE button, or touch anywhere on the right half of the screen.
CheckpointsWalk through the glowing cyan rings. Your progress is saved there and you respawn there if you die.
MissionsSome areas trigger a mission: clear the room, collect data cores, race a timer, or hold out. Gates open when the mission is done; failing sends you back to the last checkpoint.
PickupsYellow = ammo, green = health, cyan = data core.
DesktopWASD move, arrows or mouse (click to lock) look, Space fire, R recenter, Esc pause.
